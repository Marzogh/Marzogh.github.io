Drop in one or more ADIF logs and get a useful view of what actually happened: QSO totals, bands, modes, timing, possible duplicates, validation warnings and the full contact table.

Raw ADIF is excellent for moving log data between programs. It is considerably less pleasant to read yourself, which is why I made this.

Everything is parsed locally in your browser. Your log files are not uploaded anywhere.

## Limits

This is a practical parser for ordinary exported ADIF logs rather than an attempt to implement every obscure variation of the format. Duplicate matches are deliberately warnings, location fallbacks are labelled by confidence, and session grouping is only a suggested view of the data.

If you find a log it cannot read properly, send it to me and I can take a look.
