This lesson is about giving students a dependable method they can trust when solving simultaneous equations. We focus on substitution and elimination side by side so students can see that both approaches are valid, and learn when one might be quicker or cleaner than the other.

Students are guided through worked examples with an emphasis on decision-making, not just steps. Why choose elimination here, and why substitution there. That kind of thinking helps reduce the panic around forgetting a method and builds confidence across different equation types.

It is ideal for first teaching, revision, or intervention groups needing a reset with clear structure. The goal is that students leave with both accuracy and fluency, and can check their own answers with confidence.
