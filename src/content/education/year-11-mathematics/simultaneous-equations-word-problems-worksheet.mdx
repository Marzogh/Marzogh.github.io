This worksheet helps students tackle the part they usually find hardest, turning words into equations. The algebra is often manageable once the setup is right, so the lesson deliberately focuses on interpreting the context, choosing variables clearly, and building equations that actually represent the problem.

As students work through examples, they practise moving between language, equations, and meaning. We reinforce that the final answer is not just numbers on a page, but a result that has to make sense in context. That habit improves both marks and mathematical confidence.

Use this as a scaffolded class task, independent practice set, or follow-up after method lessons. It is especially useful for students who can solve equations mechanically but need support applying them in real situations.
