## Overview

A general lesson on photosynthesis inputs, outputs, cellular sites, and limiting factors.

## Learning Goals

- state and interpret the photosynthesis equation
- describe light-dependent and light-independent stages
- analyse effects of limiting factors on rate

## Worked Example

If light intensity is low, increasing carbon dioxide has limited effect because light is the limiting factor. Once light increases, CO2 may become limiting.

## Check For Understanding

- What are the raw materials and products?
- Where do photosynthetic stages occur?
- How do you identify a limiting factor from graph data?

## Common Misconceptions To Address

- plants photosynthesize only in bright sunlight
- oxygen in photosynthesis comes from carbon dioxide
- chlorophyll is a product, not a pigment

## Teaching Sequence (Suggested)

1. Start with retrieval questions from prior lessons.
2. Model one example step by step using scientific vocabulary.
3. Run guided practice with immediate feedback.
4. Move to independent application.
5. End with an exit task that requires evidence-based reasoning.

## Adaptation Notes

- Support: sentence starters, partially completed diagrams, key-term banks.
- Extension: unfamiliar data, compare-and-justify prompts, evaluate limitations.
- Assessment alignment: include one short-response and one extended-response item.
