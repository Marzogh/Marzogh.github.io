## Overview

A reusable lesson on aerobic respiration, ATP release, and links between cellular and organismal energy demand.

## Learning Goals

- state the aerobic respiration equation
- describe ATP as usable cellular energy
- connect respiration rate to tissue function

## Worked Example

During exercise, muscle ATP demand rises; aerobic respiration rate increases if oxygen supply supports mitochondrial ATP production.

## Check For Understanding

- How is respiration different from breathing?
- Why is oxygen required in aerobic pathways?
- Which tissues have highest ATP demand and why?

## Common Misconceptions To Address

- respiration occurs only in lungs
- ATP is stored in large long-term reserves
- glucose breakdown always releases energy at one constant rate

## Teaching Sequence (Suggested)

1. Start with retrieval questions from prior lessons.
2. Model one example step by step using scientific vocabulary.
3. Run guided practice with immediate feedback.
4. Move to independent application.
5. End with an exit task that requires evidence-based reasoning.

## Adaptation Notes

- Support: sentence starters, partially completed diagrams, key-term banks.
- Extension: unfamiliar data, compare-and-justify prompts, evaluate limitations.
- Assessment alignment: include one short-response and one extended-response item.
