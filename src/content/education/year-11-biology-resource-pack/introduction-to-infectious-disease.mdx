## Overview

A general lesson on pathogen classes, transmission pathways, and prevention strategies.

## Learning Goals

- distinguish infectious and non-infectious disease
- classify major pathogen types
- explain transmission modes and intervention points

## Worked Example

A respiratory virus spreads by droplets and aerosols; reducing transmission may involve ventilation, masking in high-risk settings, and isolation during peak infectious period.

## Check For Understanding

- What makes a disease infectious?
- Which transmission pathways apply in this scenario?
- Which control measure targets the weakest link in transmission?

## Common Misconceptions To Address

- all microbes are harmful
- antibiotics treat viral diseases
- infection always leads to severe symptoms

## Teaching Sequence (Suggested)

1. Start with retrieval questions from prior lessons.
2. Model one example step by step using scientific vocabulary.
3. Run guided practice with immediate feedback.
4. Move to independent application.
5. End with an exit task that requires evidence-based reasoning.

## Adaptation Notes

- Support: sentence starters, partially completed diagrams, key-term banks.
- Extension: unfamiliar data, compare-and-justify prompts, evaluate limitations.
- Assessment alignment: include one short-response and one extended-response item.
