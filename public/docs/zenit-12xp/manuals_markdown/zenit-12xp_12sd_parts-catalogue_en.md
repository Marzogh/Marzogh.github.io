# Zenit-12SD / ZENIT-12xp Parts and Assembly Catalogue

English edition rebuilt from the original Russian Zenitcamera HTML archive.

Source: `https://www.zenitcamera.com/mans/zenit-12xp/zenit-12xp-catalog.html`

![ZENIT-12sd / 12xp catalogue cover](../images/catalog/catalog_asset-001_c0.jpg)

> Source note: the original Russian page states that this text corresponds to the 1989 catalogue and was specially prepared for HTML format in 2004. The Zenitcamera HTML page also states that the written specifications are currently absent. The high-resolution 300 dpi TIFF figure plates are therefore the primary catalogue content. They are embedded below as lossless PNG derivatives for PDF compatibility, and the original TIFF files are included in the package.

## Title

ZENIT-12SD / ZENIT-12xp

Catalogue of assemblies and parts included in the photographic camera.

## Catalogue purpose

This catalogue is a technical reference aid describing the composition of the Zenit-12SD, ZENIT-12xp camera.

Each structural diagram is accompanied, in the original catalogue set, by a specification indicating the figure number, position number, name, and quantity of assemblies and parts for one product.

When ordering, indicate only the figure number, position number, and quantity.

## Notes

The Zenitcamera HTML source says that the specifications are not currently present. For the graphical TIFF files at 300 dpi, the original page recommended installing a browser plug-in such as AlternaTIFF. In this rebuilt package, the original TIFF files are retained and PDF-compatible PNG derivatives are embedded in the PDF.
