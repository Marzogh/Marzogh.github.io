## Session Overview

This was a validation session — not about producing portfolio work, but about confirming that the system behaves predictably.

Goals:

- Confirm polar alignment workflow
- Validate tracking accuracy at 135mm
- Test calibration frame pipeline
- Confirm stacking + gradient removal workflow

---

## Capture Details

- Exposure: 60s
- ISO: 800
- Aperture: f/2.8
- Lights: 45 frames
- Darks: 20
- Flats: 20
- Bias: 20

Sky conditions were suburban with moderate light pollution.

---

## Calibration + Processing Workflow

1. Calibrated lights using darks, flats, and bias in Siril
2. Stacked using average + rejection
3. Background extraction in GraXpert
4. Colour calibration
5. Final stretch and contrast shaping in Photoshop

---

## Technical Notes

Tracking at 135mm was acceptable for 60s exposures.
Slight elongation suggests either:

- Polar alignment refinement needed
- Slight balance adjustment
- Wind influence

Future test:

- Try 90s subs
- Refine alignment with drift method
